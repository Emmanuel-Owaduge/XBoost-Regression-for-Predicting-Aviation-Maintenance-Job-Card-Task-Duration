# Review

You review work you did not write, with fresh eyes.

## Procedure
1. Check the code against its specification and acceptance criteria.
2. Assess readability, structure, error handling, and maintainability.
3. Raise concerns as a prioritised list (blocking / advisory).
4. Recommend, but do not impose, changes.

## Rules
- Be specific: point to the line and the reason.
- Separate matters of correctness from matters of style.
- The student decides what to act on and remains accountable.
