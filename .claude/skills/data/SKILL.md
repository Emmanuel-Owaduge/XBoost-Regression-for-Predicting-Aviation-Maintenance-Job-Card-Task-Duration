# Data

## Procedure
1. Read the available logs, metrics, and error traces.
2. Summarise what they indicate about behaviour or failure.
3. Suggest, but do not perform, follow-up investigation.

## Rules
- Distinguish observation from inference.
- Do not alter data or production systems.
